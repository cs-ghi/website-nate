# Checklist — Spec Q-bar(\Zeta(3))

## Funding applications

- [ ] **Apply for SGS Conference Grant** — due 2026-05-23
  - Eligibility: Full-time graduate student presenting own research at a conference. Priority to students within program length. Max 3 grants per PhD.. CRITICAL: international travel (incl. USA) requires Safety Abroad registration BEFORE departure, or the grant is voided. Payment must be claimed within 30 days of the conference.
  - https://www.sgs.utoronto.ca/awards/sgs-conference-grant/

## Documents to draft

- [ ] **Draft: Talk / poster abstract**
  - A short technical abstract for the talk or poster you'd give at the conference. ~150–250 words, audience: working AG/NT researchers.
- [ ] **Draft: One-paragraph academic bio**
  - Reusable third-person bio for application forms and conference programs.
- [ ] **Draft: Statement of relevance to your research (sgs-conference-grant)**
  - SGS asks: why is this conference relevant to your research, and what will you gain from attending?
- [ ] **Draft: Budget breakdown (sgs-conference-grant)**
  - SGS requires an itemised budget for the trip.
- [ ] **Draft: Description of presentation (sgs-conference-grant)**
  - SGS requires confirmation that you are presenting. If you're not yet accepted, describe what you intend to submit.

## Travel logistics

- [ ] **Book flights and accommodation** — due 2026-05-11
  - Recommended ~6–10 weeks ahead for international, ~4 weeks for domestic. Save receipts.
- [ ] **Add to personal calendar + set OOO** — due 2026-06-08
  - Block your calendar with start/end dates and time-zone.

## Post-trip housekeeping

- [ ] **File SGS reimbursement claim** — due 2026-07-20
  - Submit receipts via SGS within 30 days of the event.
  - https://www.sgs.utoronto.ca/awards/sgs-conference-grant/
- [ ] **Write trip notes / follow-ups** — due 2026-07-09
  - Capture key talks, people met, follow-up reading. Many funders require a brief trip report.
