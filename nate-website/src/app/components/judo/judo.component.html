立技 Tachi-waza — Standing Techniques

手技 Te-waza
Hand techniques, throws executed primarily through arm and hand action, with minimal hip or leg involvement (e.g. Seoi Nage, Tai Otoshi).
腰技 Koshi-waza
Hip techniques, throws where tori's hip acts as the fulcrum to lift and project uke (e.g. Ogoshi, Harai Goshi).
足技 Ashi-waza
Foot & leg techniques, throws driven by foot sweeps, reaps, or blocks (e.g. De Ashi Barai, Osoto Gari, Uchi Mata).
捨身技 Sutemi-waza
Sacrifice techniques, tori deliberately falls to the mat to execute the throw. Ma-sutemi-waza (真捨身技) falls straight back; Yoko-sutemi-waza (横捨身技) falls to the side.
巻込技 Makikomi-waza
Winding techniques, tori wraps an arm around uke and falls with them, preventing uke from posting out (e.g. Kouchi Makikomi, Osoto Makikomi).

寝技 Ne-waza — Ground Techniques

抑込技 Osaekomi-waza
Holding / pinning techniques, tori immobilizes uke on their back for a set duration. 10–19 s = Waza-ari; 20 s = Ippon.
絞技 Shime-waza
Strangling / choking techniques, compress the carotid arteries or windpipe to force a submission (e.g. Hadaka Jime, Okuri Eri Jime, Sangaku Jime).
関節技 Kansetsu-waza
Joint locking techniques, hyperextend or rotate a joint beyond its natural range to force a tap-out. In judo, only elbow locks are legal in competition (e.g. Juji Gatame, Ude Garami).

基礎 Kiso — Fundamentals

受身 Ukemi
Breakfalling, the art of falling safely to avoid injury. The first and most essential skill in judo.
崩し Kuzushi
Off-balancing, disrupting uke's posture and balance before attempting a throw. Kuzushi → Tsukuri → Kake is the three-stage throw sequence.
作り Tsukuri
Fitting in, positioning and turning the body to set up the throw after kuzushi.
掛け Kake
Execution, the completion of the throw once in position.

General Terms

取 Tori
The person performing the technique.
受 Uke
The person receiving the technique.
乱取 Randori
Free practice, both partners attempt techniques against each other without a fixed script.
形 Kata
Formal pre-arranged forms performed with a partner, demonstrating the principles of judo technique with precision and control.
道場 Dojo
Training hall. Literally "place of the way."
礼 Rei
Bow, a sign of respect performed when entering/leaving the dojo and before/after every match or drill.
