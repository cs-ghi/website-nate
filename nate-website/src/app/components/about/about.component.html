Hi there!

I'm Nathanael Chwojko-Srawley. I am probably best known for being a little too possessed by my curiosity, being interested in almost every discipline I studied and settling for mathematics for its endless ability to provide fascinating ideas. Before mathematics, I would have labeled myself a programmer and a philosopher, writing books on my philosophical musings (a habit I converted into my mathematical career).

I firmly decided to pursue mathematics after being a software developer for a year. Software development was a natural path for me as I have been programming for years and loved the feeling of the world being "open" to you if you knew how to code and the infrastructure of comptuers. I had the incredible opportunity to join a very talented team and was trusted to work on some amazing projects that touched on so many fields of computer science. But it was this experience away from mathematics that made me realize just how much I missed it, and so I decided I must give a fair shot at pursuing it. This still felt like a compromise to my multitude of other interests; however, I am fortunate that mathematics fractals into a myriad of fields, each promising a lifetime of interesting content.

Choosing a specific field of mathematics to study was challenging. To make my decision, I decided to take as many mathematics courses as I could in my undergraduate (running out of time for combinatorics, set theory, and classical/simplicial geometry) and did research projects in different fields of mathematics during the summer; one in representation theory, one in non-linear wave equations, and one in riemann geometry. My experience was always positive, and to coalesce my interests as much as possible, I decided to pursue algebraic geometry. I am currently drawn towards Hodge Theory, Anabelian Geometry, and Elliptic Curves, but I have yet to dive into enough topics to be sure I will end up researching within these fields.
