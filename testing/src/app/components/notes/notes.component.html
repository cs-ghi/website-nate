Overview

Each document has everything I've learnt in a subject matter, from courses, textbooks and personal research, that I had time to put down. They include some of my personal discoveries in the form of exercises or remarks, and many of my intuitions. Hence, I named my series of documents "Everything You need To Know About" (EYNTKA). I update my notes on a regular basis with new information and fixes.
